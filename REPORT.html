1. ABSTRACT

The Smart Aanganbadi System is an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to replace the physical, multi-register paper records traditionally maintained by Aanganbadi workers under India's flagship Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) and POSHAN Abhiyaan schemes.

Frontline Anganwadi Workers (AWW) typically record data across more than 11 physical ledgers, consuming significant daily operational time and causing severe delays in identifying malnourished children. Furthermore, rural network conditions frequently disrupt cloud-only applications. This system bridges the gap by implementing an offline transaction queue that operates without cellular internet connectivity and automatically synchronizes when network access is restored.

The application implements the official World Health Organization (WHO) Child Growth Standards, dynamically calculating Weight-for-Age (WFA), Height-for-Age (HFA), and Body Mass Index (BMI), rendering interactive WHO growth charts, and triggering automated referral alerts for Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM). Supervisors and Child Development Project Officers (CDPO) receive multi-centre dashboards that monitor supplementary nutrition compliance and automatically generate official Monthly Progress Reports (MPR).

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & OBJECTIVES

2.1 Existing Challenges

  • Excessive Paperwork: Frontline workers spend 2 to 3 hours daily writing identical child information across multiple physical registers.
  • Delayed Malnutrition Identification: Manual paper plotting of growth trajectories fails to catch early signs of Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) and Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM).
  • Preschool Dropout Blindspots: Consecutive absences across multiple days remain unaddressed until monthly ledger compilation.
  • Unstable Rural Connectivity: Standard web applications crash or lose form submissions in rural low-bandwidth zones.

2.2 Project Objectives

  1. Provide a 1-tap mobile interface for daily attendance with batch "Mark All Present" functionality.
  2. Track Supplementary Nutrition Programme (SNP) meals, computing calories and protein in real-time against ICDS 500 kcal norms.
  3. Automate WHO Child Growth standard evaluations (-3SD to +2SD) with interactive SVG visual growth curves.
  4. Ensure 100% offline functionality through PWA Service Workers and LocalStorage queues.
  5. Provide a multi-centre supervisory analytics dashboard with automated Monthly Progress Report (MPR) generation.

4. CORE MODULES & FUNCTIONALITY

4.1 Child Enrolment & Digital Health Card

Manages child profiles including Name, Date of Birth (calculating exact age in months), Gender, Parents' Names, Aadhaar/ID, Category (6m–3y Toddler vs. 3y–6y Preschool), Blood Group, and Immunization status. Each child possesses an individual Digital Health Card tracking their nutritional status over time.

4.2 Daily Attendance & Dropout Detection

Features 1-tap toggling: Present (Green), Absent (Red), and Leave (Amber). Includes a batch "Mark All Present" shortcut. An automated detection algorithm flags children with 3 consecutive absences to trigger a home visit.

4.3 Supplementary Nutrition Programme (SNP)

Tracks three nutrition streams: Morning Snacks (Egg, Fruit, Chana), Hot Cooked Meals (Khichdi, Pulao), and Take-Home Rations (THR) for toddlers and nursing mothers. A real-time meter verifies if meals meet the government standard of 500 kcal and 12–15g protein per child.

4.4 WHO Child Growth Monitoring

Records monthly height (cm) and weight (kg). The system evaluates anthropometric parameters against standard WHO percentiles:

BMI = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]²
Z-Score = [Measured Weight - WHO Median(age, gender)] / SD(age, gender)
Classification WHO Z-Score Cutoff Clinical Protocol
Normal (Green) ≥ -2 SD Standard supplementary nutrition and growth monitoring
MAM (Yellow) -3 SD to -2 SD Dietary counseling, energy-dense foods, weekly weight checks
SAM (Red Alert) < -3 SD Immediate referral to Nutrition Rehabilitation Centre (NRC)

4.5 Pre-School Learning & Center Hygiene Check

Logs preschool sessions: Rhymes (Baal Geet), Storytelling, Drawing/Art, and Physical Play. Integrates a daily operational checklist verifying drinking water safety, first-aid readiness, handwashing demonstrations, and toilet sanitation.

4.6 Supervisor (CDPO) Hub & MPR Reports

Aggregates data across multiple centres (AW-101 to AW-104), providing comparative attendance bar charts, active SAM alert resolution tickets, and automated generation of the official Monthly Progress Report (MPR) with 1-click CSV export.

6. CONCLUSION & SOCIETAL IMPACT

The Smart Anganwadi Monitoring System provides an accessible, mobile-first digital replacement for manual paper registers. By ensuring 100% offline reliability through PWA technology and automating clinical WHO malnutrition detection, the system shortens medical intervention turnaround times from weeks to hours.

Supervisory personnel gain transparent, real-time oversight over multi-centre compliance, meal delivery, and attendance trends, significantly advancing digital health governance under the national POSHAN Abhiyaan mission.
